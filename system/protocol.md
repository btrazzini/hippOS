# hippOS memory protocol

Reader: agent. `<name>` is the person the memory belongs to (`name` in the frontmatter of `core/PROFILE.md`); memory content and replies in their `language` (same frontmatter); structure (headings, frontmatter, markers, board fields, `- none`) stays in English, as in `templates/`.

## Layers
- System: the hippOS repository, or <name>'s private copy (protocol, skills, validator, templates). Never holds memory.
- Data: the `hippOS` folder in <name>'s Google Drive, the root. Single source of memory, due dates included.

## Root in Drive
```
hippOS/
  MAP.md                   folder ids and active areas (generated by sleep)
  SLEEP-IN-PROGRESS.md     lock: exists only while a sleep runs; one sleep at a time
  core/
    PROFILE.md             who <name> is (stable); `name`, `timezone` and `language` in the frontmatter
    PREFERENCES.md         how <name> wants to be served; applies in every area
    PRINCIPLES.md          recurring decision criteria, drawn from decisions and outcomes
    STATE.md               view of all areas: due dates, waiting on <name> (generated)
    CHANGELOG.md           what each sleep changed in core and why (history)
    people/<slug>.md       people who show up in more than one area
  areas/<area>/
    AREA.md                mission, role, limits, depth, sources and lookups of this area
    BOARD.md               only what is open: phase, priorities, attention, waiting on <name> and on others, to-dos, questions, threads
    LOG.md                 dated events of the area, 30 days; not loaded at startup
    log/<YYYY-MM>.md       older events, never deleted
    STATE.md               area startup: opening, review, board, active items (generated by sleep; do not edit)
    CHANGELOG.md           what each sleep changed and why (history)
    initiatives/<slug>.md
    decisions/<YYYY-MM[-DD]>-<slug>.md
    topics/<slug>.md
    drafts/<slug>.md       texts in progress; not a source of state
  inbox/<area>/            raw captures; `inbox/core/` for what applies to every area
```
Area = folder in `areas/`. Slug = file name without `.md`, unique within the area. Reference in the body with `[[slug]]`; in frontmatter, lists without double brackets. Reference to another area (or core): `[[area:slug]]`.

## Drive access
- Root: the `hippOS` folder, directly in My Drive (fixed name). Search `title = 'hippOS'`; several: the one holding `MAP.md` (`title = 'MAP.md' and parentId = '<id>'`). Its `MAP.md`, read with `download_file_content`, lists every folder id. None: the connector is not on the Drive account that holds the memory; tell <name> and stop.
- Finding a file: always by title and folder, `title = '<file>.md' and parentId = '<folder id>'`. Several at once: `(title = 'A.md' and parentId = 'X') or (title = 'B.md' and parentId = 'Y')`. `MAP.md` (at the root) has every folder id; file ids change with every sleep and are never stored.
- Listing a folder: `pageSize: 100` and follow `nextPageToken` until empty (an exact-title search needs no token). The first page may hold only some of the files; never conclude a file does not exist before exhausting the pages.
- Reading: `read_file_content` (text may come with escaped Markdown, `\#\#`, `\_`; for sleep and any read done by a script, use `download_file_content`, which returns raw text; `MAP.md` always this way, because escaping also hits the ids). Files are plain Markdown (`text/markdown`); never convert to Google Docs.
- The connector creates files but cannot edit the content of a `.md`. So:
  - Capture: always a new file (`create_file`, `contentMimeType: text/markdown`, `disableConversionToGoogleType: true`, `parentId` of the inbox folder).
  - Curated record: only sleep changes it. Without local access, change = create the new file with the same title in the same folder, then trash the old one. Never leave two files with the same title.
- Local mode (preferred when available): with a shell, `python3 memory.py root` prints the root synced by Google Drive for desktop (Mac or Windows). If it prints one, read, search (`rg`), capture (`memory.py capture <root> <area> <origin> <topic>`, the body on stdin: it names the file, stamps the time and refuses malformed fields) and, during sleep, edit directly there; Drive syncs by itself. The ids, `MAP.md` and the search rules above apply only to connector mode.

## Frontmatter schema
- initiative: `type title status{active|paused|done|cancelled} priority{high|medium|low|tbd} owner verified(YYYY-MM-DD) next(1 line) depends_on[] unblocks[] decisions[] refs[]`
- decision: `type title status{proposed|approved|superseded|discarded} date decided_by confirmed{yes|inherited} supersedes superseded_by amends[] affects[] review_on(YYYY-MM-DD, optional)`
- `amends`: older decisions this one changes only in part (the older one stays approved; it gets in `## Addenda` the line "amended by [[slug]]: <what>"). `supersedes`: the older one stops applying entirely.
- topic: `type title updated links[]`
- person: `type title updated areas[]`
- area (`AREA.md`): `type title updated aliases[]`. Aliases are short names accepted in place of the area name (e.g., `wrk`); unique across all areas; `MAP.md` lists them.
- Dates and times always in the `timezone` of `PROFILE.md` (e.g., America/New_York).
- capture: `type area date(YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM[:SS]) origin{claude-chat|claude-code|codex|other} mode{normal|forget}`. `origin` is the product in use: `claude-chat` in the Claude app (desktop or phone), `claude-code` in Claude Code (terminal, app or cloud), `codex` in Codex.
- `confirmed`: `yes` = recorded with <name> in this memory (proposals included); `inherited` = came from another source, unconfirmed. Independent of `status`.

Body in short bullets. Initiative: `## State`, `## Risks`, `## Open`, `## Notes`. Decision: `## Context`, `## Decision`, `## Rationale`, `## Consequences`, `## Alternatives`, `## Outcome`, `## Addenda`. Topic and person: `## Facts`, `## Open`. Empty sections are omitted.

## Board items
In "Waiting on <name>" (someone waits on <name>), "Waiting on others" (what <name> waits for, as `Who: what`), "Loose ends" (<name>'s to-dos) and "Questions for <name>", one item per line:
`- <what> [markers] [[slug]] | since YYYY-MM-DD | due YYYY-MM-DD | for <who> | last YYYY-MM-DD | prep N | every month | pin`
- `since` (required): day the item entered the board. `due` (optional): one per item; `due ~YYYY-MM-DD` when approximate ("around"). Two due dates are two items. A due date never lives only in the text (the validator flags it).
- `for` (optional): the person <name> owes the item to, by the name <name> uses (e.g., `for Diego (Acme)`). Answers "what do I owe Diego?".
- `last` (optional): day of the latest nudge or news on the item; the follow-up waits 7 days from it.
- `prep N` (optional): shows the due date N days ahead, when 7 are too few (a contract notice, a speech).
- `every [n] day|week|month|year` (optional, with `due`): it recurs. `- Done:` keeps it on the board, `due` on the first occurrence after both the current `due` and the day done, `last` on the day done; `- Dropped:` ends it.
- `pin` (optional): <name> asked not to forget it; in every opening until closed.
- Section with no items: `- none`.
- `## Thread`: up to 3, one per topic, newest first: `- <topic>: <where it stopped; next step>`.
- `## Attention`: only sleep writes it, up to 5 lines, `- <what> [recommendation YYYY-MM-DD]`; the top of the opening.

## Lookups (AREA.md section)
Sources the assistant checks on its own through `lookup.py` (GitHub, via `gh`), in the format of the `## Lookups` comment in `templates/area/AREA.md`. What GitHub tells is not copied into memory: it is looked up on the spot.

## Markers (required on every statement of state)
`[fact YYYY-MM-DD: source]` checked | `[<name> YYYY-MM-DD]` said by <name> (literal in quotes, if short) | `[inherited YYYY-MM-DD: origin]` unchecked | `[hypothesis]` | `[recommendation]`. Never promote a hypothesis to fact.

## Conversation modes
- normal: capture what is worth keeping (below).
- asked not to record ("just discussing", "off the record", "don't save this"): nothing is captured until <name> releases it. Confirm in one line.
- "forget that" or "that was wrong": capture with `mode: forget` saying what; sleep removes it from the curated memory.

## Capture (every assistant session)
What to capture: decision and rationale, change of state, priority, to-do with owner and due date, risk, commitment, new domain fact, <name>'s preference on how to be served, correction made by <name>, outcome of an old decision. Do not capture: conversation without durable content, what git, gh and repositories already tell (point to them), analysis <name> did not adopt.

When: right after the fact, without waiting for the end; context may run out. Group in one capture what came up in the same stretch of conversation. Every capture ends with `## Thread`, the topic in progress (format in "Board items"): a session may end without warning.

Where: `inbox/<area>/` of the area it belongs to, whichever session it came up in; a preference or fact about <name> that applies to every area goes to `inbox/core/`, with `area: core`.

File name: `YYYYMMDD-HHMM-<origin>-<short-topic>.md` (taken: add `-2`). Content:
```
---
type: capture
area: work
date: 2026-10-28T19:55
origin: claude-chat
mode: normal
---
## Decisions
- [<name> 2026-10-28: "..."] ... (affects [[slug]])
## State
- [[slug]]: ... [fact 2026-10-28: gh pr view <n>]
## To-dos
- <name>: ... [<name> 2026-10-28] | due YYYY-MM-DD | for Diego
- Diego: ... [<name> 2026-10-28] | due YYYY-MM-DD
## Questions
- ...? [<name> 2026-10-28]
## Preferences
- ... [<name> 2026-10-28]
## Thread
- <topic>: ...
```
Only these sections; empty ones omitted. A to-do starts with whose it is. Every line with a marker, except in `## Thread`; without a marker, it counts as `[hypothesis]`. Cite the existing slug when there is one (`[[slug]]`); new topic without a slug: describe it, sleep decides where it goes. A resolved to-do: `- Done: <the item>`; given up: `- Dropped: <the item>`. An open question only <name> can answer, left for later ("I'll see"): `## Questions`, never only in `## Thread`, which sleep drops.

Read before asserting: the state of a topic is the curated record plus the pending captures in the area inbox. A pending capture that contradicts the curated record prevails, with the caveat "not yet consolidated". The current inbox listing prevails over the "Inbox not yet consolidated" section of `STATE.md`, which is a snapshot from the last sleep.

An open question ("Waiting on <name>", "Questions for <name>") never becomes a conclusion in a reply or record: only <name> closes it.

Check at the source: only when the environment reaches the source (git, gh, Sentry, local repository). Environment with only the Drive connector: state it with the record's marker and say "not rechecked since <date>".

## Lifecycle (applied by sleep)
- State rewritten in the present, never stacked. History in `CHANGELOG.md` and in Drive versions.
- Initiative leaving `active`: outcome in `## State`; the file stays.
- Approved proposal: `status: approved`, `decided_by: <name>`, `## Decision` with what was approved and `[<name> date: "words"]`; what was not approved stays as `[recommendation]` in `## Addenda`.
- An approved decision is not rewritten. Contradiction: new decision with `supersedes`; the old one gets `status: superseded` and `superseded_by`. Compatible detail: dated `## Addenda`.
- A decision with an observable effect gets `review_on`; at review, `## Outcome` with what happened. An outcome revealing a criterion of <name> becomes a line in `core/PRINCIPLES.md`.
- `confirmed: inherited`: confirm with <name> the first time the topic comes back.
- Validity: an active initiative with `verified` over 14 days old is rechecked at the source before stating its state, or declared "not rechecked since <date>".
- A closed to-do (done or dropped) leaves the BOARD, unless done with `every`, and becomes `- <the day>: Done: <the item>` in `LOG.md`, unless `mode: forget` removed it; a relevant outcome goes to the topic's file.
- An overdue to-do stays on the BOARD; the opening asks "done, postpone or drop?" and <name>'s answer becomes a capture.
- Aging: an item in "Waiting on <name>" or "Questions for <name>" without `due`, `for` or `pin` and with `since` over 14 days ago moves, fields and all, from the BOARD to `## Open` of the linked record (in a decision, `## Addenda`). Still open: the opening counts it, the weekly review lists it, only <name> closes it. Without a linked record, it stays. Every `## Open` line carries `| since`, or the review misses it.
- A dated event (delivery, release, conversation) goes to `LOG.md`, newest on top; entries over 30 days old move to `log/`, never deleted.

## Common limits (each AREA.md adds its own)
- Speak only with <name>. External action (message, issue, comment, push, calendar, email) only on request, and the request is the authorization: do it directly, without a draft or new confirmation, and say what was done, with the link.
- What <name> did not ask for (draft for third parties, issue, message): may offer it in one line; only do it after a yes.
- Recommend, don't decide. A decision holds only after <name> approves it.
- Write only inside the Drive root (and, during sleep, what this protocol allows). Never store a secret (password, key, token, card or account number) in memory.
- Stance: critical; don't agree by default; simple before process; separate fact, hypothesis, recommendation and decision.
